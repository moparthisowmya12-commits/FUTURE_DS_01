Business Sales Performance Dashboard (Task 1)
📊 Project Title

Business Sales Performance Dashboard using Power BI

📌 Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze business sales data and create an interactive dashboard that helps stakeholders monitor sales performance, profitability, product performance, and regional trends for better decision-making.

🛠️ Tools Used
Power BI Desktop
Microsoft Excel (Sample Superstore Dataset)
📂 Dataset

Dataset Name: Sample Superstore

The dataset contains information about:

Order ID
Order Date
Customer Details
Product Name
Category
Region
Sales
Profit
Quantity
Discount
📈 Dashboard Components

The dashboard includes the following visuals:

1. Total Sales
Displays the overall sales value using a Card visual.
2. Total Profit
Displays the total profit generated using a Card visual.
3. Monthly Sales Trend
A Line Chart showing how sales vary across different months.
4. Category Analysis
A Bar Chart comparing sales across product categories.
5. Regional Analysis
A Bar Chart showing sales performance across different regions.
6. Top 10 Products
A Bar Chart displaying the top-selling products based on sales.
7. Product Name Slicer
Allows users to filter the dashboard by selecting a specific product.
📊 Key Insights
Overall business generated significant sales and profit.
Technology products contributed the highest sales among categories.
The West region recorded the highest sales performance.
Sales showed fluctuations across months with peaks during the later months of the year.
A small number of products contributed a large share of total sales.
🎯 Business Benefits

This dashboard helps management to:

Monitor overall sales and profit.
Identify high-performing products and categories.
Compare regional performance.
Analyze monthly sales trends.
Make data-driven business decisions.
Improve strategic planning and inventory management.
📋 Conclusion

The Business Sales Performance Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance through interactive visualizations. It enables quick analysis of sales, profit, product performance, and regional trends, supporting informed decision-making and business growth.
